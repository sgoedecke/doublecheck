BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding

Glaring errors found mathematical-error

Findings (1)
The claimed approximately 40 corpus epochs conflicts with the stated sequence-length schedule. (minor, high confidence)
Tag
mathematical-error
Evidence type
arithmetic-error
Location
Appendix A.2 and Appendix C.1
Analysis
The 40-epoch estimate effectively counts every training sequence as length 512, although the paper states that 90% of steps use length 128.
Evidence
The stated schedule processes at most 1,000,000 * 256 * (0.9 * 128 + 0.1 * 512) = 42.5984 billion token slots. Relative to the stated 3.3 billion-word corpus, that is about 12.9 passes, not 40; tokenization differences are not large enough to close the gap.
Paper details

Authors: Jacob Devlin, Ming-Wei Chang, Kenton Lee, Kristina Toutanova

One concrete arithmetic inconsistency was identified in the stated estimate of pre-training corpus epochs.
