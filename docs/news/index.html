AI-generated label becomes mandatory in the EU euronews Errors found official-record-error major
Fact-check details

The article correctly identifies the 2 August 2026 start date and several covered uses, but it materially overstates the law by saying every AI-generated image or text must carry a user-facing label. The official EU guidance distinguishes universal machine-readable marking by providers from visible disclosure duties that apply to deepfakes and certain public-interest text.

Findings (1)
Article 50 does not require a visible label on every AI-generated image or text. Providers must add machine-readable marks to AI-generated or manipulated outputs; deployers must visibly disclose deepfakes and AI-generated public-interest text lacking human review or editorial responsibility. (major)
Article claim
when an image or text has been created using AI, there must be a label saying so
Location
Opening explanation of the rules, paragraph beginning “The EU's comprehensive AI law enters into force in stages”
Error type
official-record-error
Analysis
The European Commission’s official Article 50 guidance expressly separates providers’ machine-readable marking duty from deployers’ disclosure duty. Its exhaustive summary of deployer disclosures covers deepfakes and a limited class of public-interest text, not every AI-generated image or text. The article’s blanket user-facing-label claim therefore materially expands the official rule.
Evidence
  • Guidelines on transparency obligations for providers and deployers of certain AI systems — European Commission
    Providers must: ... Add machine-readable marks to enable the detection of AI-generated or manipulated content. Deployers ... must inform individuals when they are exposed to ... Deepfakes [and] Text publications on matters of public interest without human review or editorial control.
  • Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content — European Commission
    Section 1: Providers - Rules for marking and detection of AI-generated and manipulated content. Section 2: Deployers - Rules for labelling of deepfakes and AI-generated and manipulated text.
