Findings (1)
FRAMES begins at address 23672 ($5C78), which is IY+62 when IY is $5C3A. IY+64 is the third (most significant) byte of the three-byte FRAMES value, not its start. (minor)
Article claim
FRAMES, a 3-byte value at IY+64
Location
“Reading Keypresses and Doing I/O,” paragraph beginning “An interrupt fires every frame”
Error type
number-error
Analysis
The article itself identifies IY as $5C3A (23610 decimal). Sinclair's official system-variable table places FRAMES at 23672 and defines it as three bytes. The difference is 62 bytes, so the documented base is IY+62. The claimed IY+64 points two bytes too far into the variable.

More German than many Germans Mert Bulan - Blog & Portfolio Errors found official-record-error major
Fact-check details

One clear factual error was found in the article's description of German naturalization requirements. Other sampled claims about Baden-Württemberg's minister-president, address registration, Germany's constitutional status, and the five-year residence rule were consistent with official records.

Findings (1)
Ordinary German naturalization does not require five years of contributions to the social system. The general rule requires five years of lawful habitual residence, along with identity, qualifying residence status, livelihood, language, civic-knowledge, constitutional-commitment, and criminal-record conditions; the livelihood rule is not a five-year contribution rule. (major)
Article claim
You have to prove you paid into the social system for at least five years.
Location
“Making it official,” paragraph describing citizenship requirements
Error type
official-record-error
Analysis
Section 10 of the Nationality Act enumerates the requirements for entitlement naturalization. It requires five years of lawful habitual residence and the ability, subject to statutory exceptions, to support oneself and dependants without specified welfare benefits. It does not require five years of social-insurance contributions. The federal naturalization portal independently gives the same exhaustive overview and likewise states five years of residence, not contributions. The article therefore turns the residence period into a nonexistent contribution requirement.
Evidence
